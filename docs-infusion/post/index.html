fluid.cell: A reactive implementation supporting malleable substrates

– February 20, 2026

Submission to the March Substrates 2026 Workshop, co-located with <Programming 2026> . Updated 10th May 2026. Abstract This post explains why reactivity is a key requirement for openly authored, malleable substrates, and motivates fluid.cell , a reactive library designed to support the needs of these substrates. It preserves the good properties of widely available commodity reactive systems whilst expanding their domain. I explain the core reactive competencies of glitch freedom and early cutoff , and how fluid.cell delivers these competencies whilst supporting reactive graphs with bidirectional arcs, asynchronous propagation of reactive updates and allows the cause of these updates to be tracked to their source, and why these expanded capabilities are vital to support successful substrates.
